Project Overview

Ai Fiori is a sophisticated fine-dining restaurant located at The Langham on Fifth Avenue in New York City, offering modern interpretations of French and Italian Riviera cuisine. The website was designed to communicate the restaurant's culinary identity, atmosphere, dining experience, private events, and reservation opportunities through a highly visual digital experience.

The final website provides Ai Fiori with a visually immersive digital presence that reflects its luxury hospitality positioning and Riviera-inspired dining experience.
