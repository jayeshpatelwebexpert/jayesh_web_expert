Project Overview

El Caribe Caterers is a family-owned Brooklyn event and catering venue specializing in weddings, social celebrations, and corporate events. With more than 45 years of experience, the business needed a digital experience that could communicate its heritage, premium service, beautiful event spaces, and ability to host memorable celebrations.

The website was developed with a visually rich and elegant approach, using large photography, dark backgrounds, refined typography, and carefully structured content sections. The experience guides visitors through the venue, weddings, social events, corporate offerings, gallery, and contact journey while maintaining a strong luxury-event aesthetic.

My Contribution

I was responsible for the WordPress development and front-end implementation of the website, translating the approved visual direction into a responsive and engaging digital experience. My focus was on creating accurate layouts, smooth interactions, responsive behaviour, and a consistent experience across desktop and mobile devices.
