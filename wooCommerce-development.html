My Design Process

01

Discovery

I understand your business, target audience, competitors, goals, and project requirements.

02

Strategy

I define the information architecture, content structure, user flow, and overall design direction.

03

Wireframing

I create wireframes to establish page structure, hierarchy, navigation, and user experience before moving into visual design.

04

UI Design

I transform the approved structure into a polished interface using typography, colors, spacing, imagery, and visual hierarchy.
