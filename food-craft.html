Project Overview

Aurelia Interiors is a premium interior design website concept created for luxury residential and commercial interior design studios. The goal of this design was to communicate elegance, sophistication, and trust through a refined visual language while providing an intuitive browsing experience.

The interface blends modern minimalism with timeless typography and warm earthy tones to create a premium digital presence that reflects the quality of interior design services.

Design Goals

  • Create a premium luxury brand experience
  • Showcase interior projects beautifully
  • Maintain a clean editorial-style layout
  • Improve content readability
  • Highlight services with a clear visual hierarchy
  • Encourage consultation bookings through strategic CTAs
  • Deliver a modern desktop-first user experience

Components

  • Navigation Bar
  • Primary Button
  • Project Cards, Service Cards, Statistics Cards, CTA Banner and Image Cards
  • Footer
  • Grid Layout

Design Highlights

  • Luxury-inspired visual identity
  • Editorial-style typography
  • Spacious and balanced layouts
  • Warm neutral color palette
  • Strong visual hierarchy
  • Large immersive imagery
  • Consistent spacing system
  • Premium card-based interface
  • Clear conversion-focused CTAs
  • Elegant user experience

Project Gallery

Design Outcome

The final design delivers a sophisticated and premium digital experience that reflects the elegance of a luxury interior design studio. Through refined typography, carefully balanced layouts, and a cohesive visual system, the concept creates a strong brand impression while guiding users naturally toward exploring projects and booking consultations.
