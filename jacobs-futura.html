Project Overview

Jacobs Futura Foundation is a philanthropic organization dedicated to creating long-term social impact through initiatives in education, environmental sustainability, athlete development, and community support.

The objective of this project was to build a modern, inspiring, and trustworthy digital platform that effectively communicates the foundation's mission, highlights its initiatives, and encourages public engagement through stories, reports, and impact-driven content.

The website combines elegant typography, editorial-style layouts, and structured storytelling to create a professional experience that reflects the organization's values.
